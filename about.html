About

Grassroots Conversations is a practical set of tools for partner clubs and the ground network. It supports the work of the Yavapai County Republican Committee under Sec 1.1 purposes: building precinct capacity, supporting Precinct Committeemen, and increasing local turnout through neighbors talking to neighbors.

Mission

Equip volunteers with short, usable conversation tools so that personal bridges can help neighbors cover neighbors—one block or one person at a time. The frame is simple: return to the original social media.

How it fits

  • Aligned with YavGOP bylaws and Sec 1.1 purposes.
  • Supports the Partner Clubs + Ground Network model and PC Partner Reps.
  • Listen-first on local issues. Match the ask to the setting.
  • Always close with ownership: We are the they.

Everyone on the path is part of the grassroots team supporting the ground network group—equipping personal bridges so neighbors can cover neighbors.
