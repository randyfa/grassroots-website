Partners & Ground Network

The Partner Clubs + Ground Network model equips club liaisons, personal bridges, and PC Partner Reps so neighbors can cover neighbors.

How it works

  • Club liaisons connect partner clubs to the ground network.
  • PC Partner Reps support club liaisons and personal bridge members on a voluntary, light basis.
  • Everyone on the path is part of the grassroots team supporting the ground network group.
  • Tracking stays simple and tied to the existing commitment log (one person / one block).

Match the ask to the setting

Republican club rooms can go deeper. Pure civic or mixed settings stay local and nonpartisan in tone. Always return to one practical next step and the “We are the they” close.
