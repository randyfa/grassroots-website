About

I started door knocking in 2019, and I’ve learned a lot since then — 3,000 homes in Scottsdale, then here in Prescott, then training people across the state. This is the method I use now: listen first, talk from the heart, and treat people like neighbors, not opponents.

Mission

We want to help volunteers have real conversations with neighbors. Listen first. Talk from the heart. One person at a time. The frame is simple: return to the original social media — neighbors talking to neighbors.

How it fits

  • Listen first. Find out what’s affecting their family.
  • Match the ask to the setting. A club room can go deeper; a porch stays short and local.
  • Close with one practical next step.
  • Always close with ownership: We are the they.
