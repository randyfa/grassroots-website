Background: understanding the people you talk to

These pages are for volunteers, not for the door. Some help you understand why people feel the way they do. One gives background on a long American tradition of building, so you can connect a conversation to local results. In all of them, listen first, have empathy, and bridge appropriately. Do not bring these topics up as a lecture.
