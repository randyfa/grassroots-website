We Are the They

When someone says “nobody is doing anything” about empty precincts or low turnout:

“I hear you. When we wait for them, three fingers point back at us. We are the they. What’s the one block or the one person you’ll cover this month?”

Simple next steps you can offer

  • Check your own voter registration and help one neighbor do the same.
  • Attend one short PC orientation or Vottiv training.
  • Cover one empty street or block in your precinct this month.
  • Become a Precinct Committeeman (or help recruit one).

Different rooms, different permission

  • Republican club: Direct asks for PC service or walking are fine.
  • Civic club: Stick to registration dates, deadlines, and nonpartisan ballot information.
  • Church or 501(c)(3): Strictly nonpartisan. Save any party conversation for private follow-up.
