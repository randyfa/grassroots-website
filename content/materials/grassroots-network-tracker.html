How to Use This Tracker

Simple tracking for clubs and the personal bridges their members already have in the community. Frame: Grassroots Conversations — Return to the original social media, neighbors talking to neighbors. Variations: “neighbors talking to neighbors — next neighbor, next person” and “neighbors covering neighbors.” Pair with the “We are the they” close.

Club Roster

  • One row per Republican club.
  • Primary Contact is the single liaison (usually the president).
  • Update Last Contact Date and Next Action after every touchpoint.
  • Community Groups Identified? = Yes once the club has named 3–5 groups and bridge members.

Grassroots Bridges

  • One row per bridge member / grassroots group combination.
  • Status examples: Identified / Materials Delivered / Active / Follow-up Needed / Completed.
  • Outcome / Commitment captures the “next neighbor / next person” or voter registration action.
  • Follow-up Date drives the next contact.

Cadence

  • One volunteer keeps the master copy and updates it after each liaison contact.
  • Clubs may keep a short local list and report monthly.

Success indicators

  • Every club has a primary liaison and has identified at least one grassroots group.
  • Bridge members are using the conversation model (“We are the they”).
  • Personal commitments are recorded and followed up.

Keep filled-in copies private. They hold people’s contact details.
