* Treat Independent, PND, and Other as the same practical unaffiliated pool for conversation purposes.  
* Keep base turnout and PC coverage primary. Do not build a separate Independent / PND / Other program.  
* Use the open-primary fact only when relevant (primary season or when the person asks how unaffiliated voters can participate).  
* Always close back to one local commitment and the “We are the they” frame.  
* Match the ask to the setting.
